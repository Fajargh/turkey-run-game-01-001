**Gobble Dash — The Great Turkey Escape.** Thanksgiving is coming, and one turkey refuses to be dinner. Sprint through the farmyard, the barn and the kitchen — jump fences and hay bales, duck under low branches and thrown carving knives, leap the glowing oven — while the farmer chases you with his pitchfork. Grab corn for points, golden feathers for bonuses and pumpkin pie for extra lives, and reach the forest gate 1500 m away to escape.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and play with keyboard and mouse: **Space / click** to jump, **↓** to duck, **→** to sprint, **P** to pause, **M** to mute. The game is plain static files under `game/` — no build step, no network request, all art and sound drawn and synthesized in code.
